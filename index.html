Common questions

Where is my data?

On your devices and in your own private iCloud. Mac and iPhone stay in sync through iCloud. Nobody else — including the developer — can see it.

Sync doesn’t work.

Make sure you are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on both devices and that iCloud Drive is turned on. Jole shows the sync status in Settings.

How does the AI work?

Smart Capture uses Apple Intelligence on your device. Your text never leaves the device. Without Apple Intelligence, Jole uses its built-in rules.

Can I export my data?

Yes — on the Mac in Admin → Backup & Data, as a complete backup you can restore at any time.
